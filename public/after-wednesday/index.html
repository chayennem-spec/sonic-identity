WHY MUSIC

THE BRAIN STRENGTHENS WHAT IT REPEATS. MUSIC MAKES REPEATING EFFORTLESS.

You already know new patterns take practice. Here's what the research says about why music is such an easy way to practice them.

MUSIC SHIFTS STATE

A review of 104 randomized controlled trials with 9,617 people found music reduced both the body's stress response and how stressed people felt.

de Witte et al., Health Psychology Review, 2020

A MELODY HELPS WORDS STICK

Words sung to the same repeated melody were remembered better than the same words spoken. Same tune, every time, is the point.

Wallace, Journal of Experimental Psychology, 1994

MUSIC HELPS MOMENTS STICK

Music strengthens memory for what happens while it plays, through the brain's reward system.

Ripollés et al., 2018

The honest part: this research is about music in general, not about After Wednesday or any one practice. It's why I'm building it, not a promise of results.

QUESTIONS

ASK ME ANYTHING.

The honest answers to what practitioners usually wonder first.

How is this different from relaxation or meditation music?+

Most of that music is made to help someone unwind, and it usually has no words. These are full songs with the words of change sung right into them, so your client has something specific to come back to between sessions.

How will my clients get the music?+

On their phone. You share a track with them, they press play. Whether that happens through an app, a membership or both is being decided with the waitlist right now.

Is this AI music?+

It’s custom-made by me, with AI as one of the tools I use, the way a photographer uses a camera. The choices that matter, like the pacing, the words and the moment a track lands, come from my own background in hypnosis and NLP.

Does it replace my sessions, or therapy?+

Not at all. Your sessions are where the shift happens, and nothing replaces you there. It isn’t therapy or clinical treatment of any kind. It’s music to support the work you already do.
